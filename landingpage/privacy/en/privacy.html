RadRecon data protection notice

RadRecon stores survey data on the device where you recorded it. The app does not require an account and does not upload the local database to any RadRecon cloud or back-end system.

Data stored on the device

Records may contain survey sessions, measurement values and units, timestamps, notes, GPS coordinates and accuracy, instrument and radiation source data, names and concentrations of chemical and biological substances, sample analysis results, and settings. The app accesses location data only after you grant permission and use a feature that needs it.

Files attached to surveys also remain on the device: photos, screenshots, spectrum files, floor plans, attachments of sample analyses, and figures added to the protocol.

The person register may contain name, phone number, e-mail address, identifier and address. If a person is recorded as injured in a survey, sex, age, weight and height may be recorded too, and the records of injured persons may include injuries, illnesses and contamination marked on a body map (isotope, chemical or biological substance). This is health-related, particularly sensitive data. The legal basis for recording it, informing the persons concerned and the conditions for passing it on are the responsibility of the organisation carrying out the survey.

Storage and retention

The app uses a local SQLite database. Data stays on the device until you delete it or delete the app's data. You can archive a closed survey; archiving does not delete data. Operating system backups may copy app data, depending on your settings. Exported files and company backup files are separate copies; the company backup file contains the full database and the attached files.

The app does not encrypt the database, backup files or exports. Use the operating system settings to lock and encrypt the device, and keep backup files and exports safe.

Network connections

When a map is displayed, OpenStreetMap may receive your IP address, the coordinates of the requested map tiles and usual request data; the tiles can reveal which area you are viewing. The app fetches radrecon.hu/version.json at most once a day to check for updates; the server then sees your IP address. The volunteer form opens an external (Google Forms) page. If a Modbus/TCP instrument is configured, the app communicates with the address you entered. Location, notification, file selection and sharing features use operating system services.

Export and choices

The app contains no analytics or advertising SDK, and the developer does not receive measurement data. You choose where exported files are saved or shared. The Word or PDF protocol may include measurement points, the identifiers of surveyors and injured persons, sex, age, weight, height, injuries and contamination of injured persons, sample analysis results and body map figures. The address is not included in the protocol, but the person register CSV export includes the identifier and the address. Give the finished protocol and export files only to persons authorised to receive them. External applications, map providers, instruments and platforms follow their own rules.

Rights and contact

With the features and device settings available, you can view, change, export or delete the data. You manage persons' data on the person's record, in the injured person's sheet of a survey and in the person list. You can disable or withdraw location permission in the device settings. For questions: info@radrecon.hu.
