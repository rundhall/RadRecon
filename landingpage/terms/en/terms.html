3. The App

RadRecon is a tool for documenting field radiation surveys. It records measurement points with GPS position, timestamp, instrument and photos, shows them on a map, offers calculation tools and exports reports and data files. The App works on your device and has no user accounts. Features may change, be added or be removed in updates.

RadRecon is a documentation and calculation aid. It is not a measuring instrument, a dosimeter or a certified safety device, and it does not replace qualified radiation protection experts, calibrated instruments or the procedures required by the competent authority.

  • You are responsible for the correctness of every value you enter or read in, for the calibration and fitness of the instruments you use, and for compliance with the radiation protection rules that apply to you.
  • Calculated values (activity, decay, dose rate, estimated activity) are reference values based on standard gamma constants and half-lives. Verify them against an authoritative source before you use them in an official record.
  • The direction suggested by the source search is a simple gradient estimate from the points recorded so far. It can be inaccurate and is for guidance only. Never base safety decisions on it. Follow the instructions of your radiation protection officer.
